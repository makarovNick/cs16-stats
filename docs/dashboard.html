CS 1.6
Server Census We polled the entire public list of Counter-Strike 1.6 servers over the Valve A2S protocol. We found 112 185 "servers" — and discovered that almost all of it is inflation.

After 25 years the game hasn't died — but its "population" has long lived by the rules of inflating trackers. 3738 real machines, 205 of which use port farms to balloon the count into the hundreds of thousands. Below is what's really going on.

The main story

The inflation funnel

From the raw number of addresses to how many truly independent servers there are. Each step strips away one layer of inflation. Bar width is the share of the original 112 185.

The one same name "ROMANIA.FULLBOOST.RO V.I.P FREE" showed up 3929 times — one server cloned across 3929 ports. Counting that as three thousand servers is exactly the mistake public trackers make.
Concentration

205 addresses against everyone else

A port farm is a single machine answering A2S on dozens and hundreds of ports. Just 205 such IPs hold 94% of all records in the dataset.
